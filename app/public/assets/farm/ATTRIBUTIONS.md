# Smart Farm visual assets

These resources are bundled locally; the application does not contact the
sources at runtime.

| Files | Creator / source | License |
|---|---|---|
| `cow.glb` | Innerscene Corp, [Dairy cow](https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/3d-parts/dairy-cow-cfd4017e) | [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/), also embedded in the GLB |
| `sheep.glb` | Innerscene Corp, [Low-poly Farm - Sheep](https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/3d-parts/low-poly-farm-sheep-91c0e0b5) | CC0 1.0, also embedded in the GLB |
| `soil.jpg`, `soil-normal.jpg` | Rob Tuytel / Poly Haven, [Brown Mud 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/brown_mud_02), 1K diffuse and OpenGL normal maps | [CC0](https://polyhaven.com/license) |

The original files are unmodified. At runtime the animal meshes are centered,
scaled and merged by material; cattle coat colors are changed to distinguish
the illustrative herds. The models are not anatomically accurate depictions of
the named breeds. Grass and concrete textures are shared with Smart Energy;
their sources are documented in [its attribution file](../energy/ATTRIBUTIONS.md).
